# Thimoli — prompts mascotte

Mode utilisé : ImageGen intégré, édition avec préservation d’identité à partir de `deck_assets/mascotte-thimoli-v1.png`.

Contraintes communes : conserver exactement le visage, la coiffure, la carnation, l’âge, les proportions et la tenue traditionnelle de la référence ; rendu d’application mobile Flat Design / Soft UI avec légère profondeur 2.5D, matières mates et silhouette nette ; personnage entier dans le cadre ; véritable fond transparent ; aucun texte, bulle, logo, décor, accessoire, membre coupé, doigt supplémentaire, photoréalisme, forte brillance 3D ou filigrane.

## Indice

Redraw the mascot from the reference giving a helpful tip, with one index finger gently raised and his head slightly tilted. Keep a calm encouraging smile, curious eyebrows, and make him look slightly upward toward where an interface speech bubble will appear.

## Réussite

Redraw the mascot from the reference reacting to a correct answer with a clear thumbs-up, a joyful smile and a slight forward lean. Keep a proud but friendly expression, bright attentive eyes and an energetic positive posture.

## Correction douce

Redraw the mascot from the reference offering a kind correction, with one open palm calmly presenting the explanation and the other hand lightly near his heart. Keep a small reassuring smile, empathetic eyes and relaxed eyebrows; supportive and never disappointed. Avoid sadness, anger, shame and red error symbols.

## Célébration

Redraw the mascot from the reference celebrating a completed lesson, raising both arms above shoulder level with a joyful smile and one foot slightly lifted in a playful victory pose. Keep the full body visible and avoid embedded confetti or trophies.

## Nettoyage de transparence

Remove only the baked checkerboard background and replace it with genuine alpha transparency. Preserve the character pixel-faithfully: face, hair, expression, pose, hands, clothing, colors, shadows and edges must remain unchanged. Do not redraw, crop, add text or add scenery.

L’état `welcome` reprend directement la mascotte maîtresse, déjà dans une pose de salut adaptée à l’accueil.
